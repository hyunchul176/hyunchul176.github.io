Hyunchul Park

Ph.D. student, Cho Chun Shik Graduate School of Mobility, KAIST.

I am a Ph.D. student at KAIST advised by Prof. Inhi Kim in the TUPA Lab. I work on autonomous-vehicle safety evaluation, V2X-aware humanoid drivers, and human-centric traffic safety research, studied with immersive VR experiments.

hyunchul.park@kaist.ac.kr

Research interests

Autonomous vehicle safety evaluation

Simulation-based stress testing, including RL-trained adversarial agents.

V2X-aware humanoid drivers

Humanoid robots that drive using V2X to participate as new traffic agents.

Human-centric traffic safety research

VR experiments on how drivers and pedestrians decide in safety-critical settings.
