2025

  • Dec 2025 Q1 Paper Publication.
    I am excited to share that my paper has been accepted for publication in a top-tier journal, Transportation Research Part F: Traffic Psychology and Behaviour. The paper, titled "Micro Mobility Safety Challenges: A Study on Drivers Overtaking Bicycles and E-Scooters in Relation to Road Conditions and Prior Riding Experience," tackles critical safety issues regarding the interaction between motor vehicles and micro-mobility.
  • Aug 2025 The 2nd IPESK Young Investigator Award in Engineering.
    I am honored to have received the 2nd IPESK Next-Generation Engineering Researcher Award, which recognizes outstanding graduate students at global research-oriented universities for their academic excellence and research achievements. This award not only acknowledges my recent contributions but also provides unique opportunities such as participation in industry-academic- research projects, career development support, and international recognition. I am deeply grateful for this recognition and will continue striving to grow as a researcher who contributes to both academia and society.
  • Aug 2025 LTWG Meeting in Cambodia. From August 12 to 14, 2025, I had the opportunity to attend the ASEAN Land Transport Working Group (LTWG) Meeting in Cambodia. The LTWG serves as ASEAN's key working group dedicated to advancing the land transport sector across member states. At this meeting, we presented updates on two important initiatives carried out with the ASEAN Secretariat: a Gap Analysis Study on the Implementation of Digitalization of Public Transport and Last-Mile Delivery Services in ASEAN Member States, and the ASEAN–ROK Transport Cooperation Roadmap. Since these are projects I am directly leading, I was able to take on the trip with greater engagement and responsibility. Experiencing ASEAN countries firsthand — beyond the reports, images, and texts I had worked with before — allowed me to gain new perspectives and insights that will strengthen the way I approach these tasks. The digitalization study is set to be completed this year, while the Roadmap project is now entering a more active implementation phase.
  • Feb 2025 M.S. Graduation.
    I received my M.S. degree from the Cho Chun Shik Graduate School of Mobility, KAIST, and am continuing to my Ph.D. in the same lab under the guidance of Prof. Inhi Kim.
  • Jan 2025 2025 TRB Outstanding Paper Award.
    I'm thrilled to announce that our paper, "Micromobility Safety Challenges: A Study on Drivers Overtaking Bicycles and E-Scooters in Relation to Road Conditions and Prior Riding Experience," presented at the 104th TRB Annual Meeting, has been honored with the Outstanding Paper Award by the TRB Committee on Bicycle Transportation (ACH20). The paper received a perfect score of 10/10, achieving the Highest Presentation Score among the submissions. This recognition underscores the significance and quality of our research in advancing the safety of micromobility users.
  • Jan 2025 104th TRB Annual Meeting Oral Presentation.
    I'm pleased to share that I delivered an oral presentation at the 104th TRB Annual Meeting on the topic, "Micro Mobility Safety Challenges: A Study on Drivers Overtaking Bicycles and E-Scooters in Relation to Road Conditions and Prior Riding Experience." This marks my very first oral presentation at the TRB, making it an especially meaningful milestone in my research journey.
  • Jan 2025 Q1 Paper Publication as a 2nd Author.
    I'm delighted to announce that the paper I co-authored as the second author, "The Impact of Information Delivery Systems in Tunnels Depending on Lighting Intensity and Speed Limit," has been published in the prestigious Q1 journal Tunnelling and Underground Space Technology (IF: 6.7).

2024

  • Oct 2024 2024 KAIST Graduate Student Outstanding Paper Award.
    I am honored to receive the 2024 KAIST Graduate Student Outstanding Paper Award, presented by the President of KAIST. This award, which recognizes high-impact research publications, is granted to students who have published in journals with significant impact factors. My recent paper, titled "Effects of Driver's Braking Behavior by the Real-Time Pedestrian Scale Warning System," was published in Accident Analysis and Prevention (IF: 5.7) and led to this recognition. The award also includes a prize of 1.5 million KRW, encouraging further contributions to advancing global research.
  • Oct 2024 36th ICTCT Conference.
    I attended the ICTCT conference on October 17–18, 2024, in The Hague, Netherlands. At the conference, I gave an oral presentation on my research titled "Micro Mobility Safety Challenges: A Study on Drivers Overtaking Bicycles and E-Scooters in Relation to Road Conditions and Prior Riding Experience." I also participated in the 2024 Traffic Safety Researcher's Course, which was an excellent opportunity to deepen my knowledge and connect with global traffic safety researchers. The conference was a valuable experience, providing new insights and inspiration for my work in traffic safety.
  • Jul 2024 Young-Han Kim Global Leadership Scholarship.
    I am thrilled to announce that I have been selected as a recipient of the Global Leadership Scholarship. This highly competitive scholarship is awarded annually to only 25 outstanding students at KAIST, with just 16 spots allocated to those in the College of Engineering. The Global Leadership Scholarship recognizes exceptional academic excellence, research capabilities, and leadership qualities. As part of this honor, I will receive an academic encouragement fund of 4 million KRW per year. This scholarship not only acknowledges my past accomplishments but also supports my future endeavors in academic and research pursuits.
  • Jul 2024 Master's Student Research Funding.
    I have been awarded the Graduate Student Research Encouragement Fund by the National Research Foundation (NRF). I will carry out the research titled "Developing an Aggressive Pedestrian Model for Autonomous Vehicle Safety Testing" from July of this year until June of the next year, with a total research budget of 12 million won. The Graduate Student Research Encouragement Fund is a newly initiated program this year designed to support the research of graduate students with creative and challenging ideas.
  • Jun 2024 Q1 Paper Publication.
    In June 2024, I published a paper as the first author in the Q1 SSCI journal Accident Analysis and Prevention (Impact Factor: 5.7). The paper is titled "Effects of Driver's Braking Behavior by the Real-Time Pedestrian Scale Warning System." This research examines the impact of real-time pedestrian warning systems on drivers' braking behavior, contributing valuable insights to the field of traffic safety.
  • Apr 2024 Best Teaching Assistant Award.
    As a teaching assistant for the Fall 2023 semester's Intelligent Transportation Systems Special Topics course, focusing on "Experiments in Future Mobility within the Metaverse," I facilitated various mobility experiments and practical sessions. Contributing to the course, I utilized KAIST's virtual environment for drone scanning and rendering. In recognition of my efforts, I received the Best Teaching Assistant Award.
  • Feb 2024 President's Award from KST.
    In recognition of outstanding academic performance and promising potential in the field of transportation studies, I was selected as a distinguished graduate and received the 15th Korean Society of Transportation President's Award.
  • Jan 2024 TRB 103rd Annual Meeting.
    As the second author, we presented a paper at the 2024 Transportation Research Board (TRB) 103rd Annual Meeting in Washington, D.C. The paper is titled "The Impact of Information Delivery Systems in Tunnels Depending on Lighting Intensity and Speed Limit."
