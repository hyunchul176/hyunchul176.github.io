World-Model-Based Driving Skill Learning for Humanoid Robots and Real-Vehicle Validation in a V2X Testbed

Personal NRF · Sep 2026 – Aug 2028

A personal NRF-funded project that I lead as principal investigator. Autonomous driving has advanced by making the vehicle itself intelligent; this project takes the opposite route and replaces the driver, so that an ordinary car with no autonomous hardware can still be driven autonomously by a humanoid robot operating its steering wheel and pedals. The first phase couples a robot simulator with a driving simulator, learns a world model that represents the robot's joint control and the vehicle's response together, and derives a driving policy through reinforcement learning inside that model, widening the task from straight roads to curves and then intersections. The second phase transfers the policy to a real Unitree G1 humanoid, checks the sim-to-real gap through hardware-in-the-loop experiments, and runs the robot in a real vehicle at the KAIST Munji campus V2X testbed, where cooperative messages such as recommended intersection speeds are received and executed by the humanoid driver rather than by the vehicle controller.

World Model Humanoid Driving Reinforcement Learning V2X

Development of Virtual Environment and Demonstration Technology for Automated Driving based on Metaverse

Lab MOLIT · 2023 – 2027

A long-running national research program building a metaverse-based virtual environment for validating automated-driving technology. My work in this project focuses on behaviorally realistic traffic agents (pedestrians, cyclists, and other vehicles) that can stress- test autonomous-vehicle decision-making in scenarios that are impractical to reproduce on real roads. This project provides the simulation infrastructure for several of my ongoing studies on reinforcement-learning–based adversarial agents and humanoid-driven vehicle interactions.

ASEAN–Korea Transport Cooperation Roadmap (2026–2030)

Lab MOLIT · 2024 – 2025

Working with the ASEAN Secretariat, I led the planning of the ASEAN–Korea transport cooperation roadmap for 2026–2030, identifying five-year priorities across digitalization of public transport, last-mile delivery, and capacity-building among ASEAN member states. The roadmap was presented and discussed at the ASEAN Land Transport Working Group meeting in Cambodia in August 2025.

ASEAN Transport Policy Digitalization International Cooperation

Developing an Aggressive Pedestrian Model for Autonomous Vehicle Safety Testing

Personal NRF · Jul 2024 – Feb 2025

A personal NRF-funded project to design and validate an "aggressive pedestrian" behavior model for stress-testing autonomous vehicle safety. Most existing pedestrian models in AV simulators are conservative and rule-following; this project instead built a behavioral model that explicitly captures risk-taking and rule-violating crossing patterns, providing a sharper test of an AV's response to vulnerable road users.

Autonomous Vehicles Pedestrian Modeling Safety Testing Adversarial Behavior
